# Formal verification strategy

Lean complements the search programs; it does not replace them. The project
uses four explicitly different evidence levels:

| Level | Meaning | Current example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lean kernel theorem | An abstract mathematical statement is checked from pinned source, with its axioms printed | Hurwitz product, inverse, braid, conjugation, and generated-subgroup lemmas |
| Lean compact certificate | Kernel reduction checks a committed finite certificate generated from a computational artifact | test 0058 degree-23 tuples, Hurwitz edges, connectivity, and conjugation assignments |
| Independently replayed computation | Separate GAP/Python/C++ paths check a large result impractical to reduce in the kernel | the completed 4A bounded search |
| Published-theorem boundary | A precisely cited theorem connects finite data to geometry or arithmetic not formalized here | the branch-cycle interpretation of complex conjugation |

`Lean kernel theorem` does not mean axiom-free foundations. The audit prints
the standard assumptions used by Lean/mathlib, currently `propext`,
`Classical.choice`, and `Quot.sound` where applicable. It means that project
sources introduce no axioms or unfinished proofs and do not use the native
decision shortcut.

## Trust policy

- Pin Lean and mathlib, including the resolved dependency manifest.
- Reject `sorry`, `admit`, project `axiom` declarations, and `native_decide` in
  the kernel-checked source tree.
- Compile `M23Formal/Audit.lean` on every check so the assumptions of headline
  theorems are visible.
- Generate finite Lean data from the committed source artifact and compare it
  byte-for-byte before compiling.
- State the unformalized boundary in each certificate file and experiment note.

## Current formal kernel

For an arbitrary group, not just `M23`, Lean checks:

- the project's adjacent Hurwitz move preserves the adjacent product;
- its stated inverse is a two-sided inverse;
- adjacent moves satisfy the Artin braid relation;
- Hurwitz moves commute with simultaneous conjugation;
- an adjacent move preserves the subgroup generated by the pair;
- prefix-conjugated inversion sends the tuple product to its inverse;
- product-one tuples remain product one; and
- prefix-conjugated inversion is exactly involutive.

For test 0056, a generator reads
`data/derived/m23_8a_small_component_descent.tsv` and emits the two finite Lean
tables. Kernel reduction proves that the 14-component action is an involution
with exactly two fixed components and that the 28 symmetry-orbit action is a
fixed-point-free involution.

Test 0058 goes below that summary. GAP exports all 168 canonical degree-23
tuples, 504 adjacent-Hurwitz edges, and 168 prefix-inversion images, including
an explicit simultaneous-conjugating permutation for every equality. Lean
checks that all permutation tables are bijections, every tuple is product one
with four order-eight entries, the 168 canonical tuples are distinct, all
edges hold, every twelve-vertex graph is connected, and every conjugation
assignment agrees with the test-0056 component map.
The generated test-0059 transporters additionally let Lean check that all
fourteen ordered degree-12 braid actions are simultaneously conjugate. The
test-0061 extension checks exact order 2 or 4 for all 1,008 pair products and
proves that the transporters preserve the resulting six boundary labels at
every vertex. It also checks that none of the 168 tuples splits into two
inverse pairs, supporting the theorem-boundary audit in test 0062.

For tests 0099 and 0100, `formal/M23Formal/Certificates/Test0099.lean` checks only
the finite deduction applied to externally supplied inputs: the two GAP
component sizes and inverse-pair counts add up to the certified totals, and a
permutation of a two-element component set with a fixed point is the
identity. Seguin's theorem and the GAP census remain outside Lean.

## Deliberate current boundary

The tuple-level certificate treats the supplied objects as permutations of 23
points. It does not yet prove inside Lean that their generated permutation
group is `M23`, or that its order-eight elements lie in the ATLAS class `8A`;
those facts remain independently replayed GAP checks. It also does not
formalize the geometric branch-cycle theorem identifying prefix-conjugated
inversion with complex conjugation on covers.

None of the headline results (the bridge counts, the bounded 4A search, the
two negative 8A results, or the König genus-11 obstruction) is checked in
Lean. Lean checks only the abstract Hurwitz algebra above and the finite steps
of tests 0056, 0058 (with its 0059/0061 extensions), and 0099.

## Reproduction

Run:

```sh
make check-lean
```

The first build must materialize the pinned mathlib dependency and may use its
precompiled cache. Subsequent checks are local and incremental.
